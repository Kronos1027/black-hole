# Exp129 — Adaptive reversible RGB transform selection (2026-11-17)

Independent local experiment, continuing Exp126–128 without repeating per-channel predictor sweep. Tested six reversible modes: raw interleaved, raw planar, up planar, green planar, green+up planar, green+left planar, all with LZMA-6 and full 13-byte BLKH header. Four development and four holdout images from skimage, RGB resized to 128x128. Every one of 48 BLKH encode/decode combinations reconstructed exactly; WebP lossless and PNG also decoded exactly.

Selection was frozen using development only: global green+up planar; adaptive per-image selection with a one-byte overhead did not win on development. Development: BLKH 103304 bytes, WebP 89746, PNG 118216. Holdout: BLKH **78588 bytes**, WebP lossless **69024 bytes**, PNG 98563. BLKH is **13.86% larger than WebP** on holdout; negative result. Compared with Exp128 (86816 bytes), this is an improvement, but not a competitive discovery; effectively matches Exp126 (78596 bytes) within container-header difference. No claims of superiority versus PNG, no rate-distortion curves or original-resolution photographic evaluation.
