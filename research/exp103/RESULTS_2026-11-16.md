# Exp103: local real-image smoke benchmark, 2026-11-16

Research branch: `research/exp103-adaptive-predictor-prototype`. Actual local run on Linux, Python 3.13.5, Pillow 12.3.0, libwebp 1.6.0; bundled scikit-image sample images, converted to 8-bit grayscale where needed. Prototype includes its 13-byte header; PNG/WebP/JPEG2000 are full encoded files. All decoders reproduced the exact pixel bytes; prototype roundtrip checked against original. Unit tests: **2 passed**.

**Negative result:** prototype loses to PNG and WebP lossless on all 5 images, and to JPEG2000 on 4/5. These are real images bundled in a library, but not an independent unseen benchmark; do not claim scientific generalization. Encoding includes 4 candidate predictors, all using zlib level 9; timings are one-shot and do not measure peak RSS or baseline codec timing. No AVIF or JPEG XL codec available in this local environment, so neither was measured. The lossless prototype cannot fairly claim rate-distortion advantage over lossy modes. The workflow added in this branch has **not yet been observed passing**; run it and inspect artifact.
